- Bookkeeping Services: Real-time automated books
          - Inventory Management: InventoryIQ, multi-warehouse SKUs
          - Tax Services: Federal/state tax prep, extensions, CPA support
          - Fractional CFO: Dedicated CFO support, strategic financial guidance
